# Dudh Sagar — Dairy Delivery & Management System

> **A simple and practical dairy delivery management system for organizing daily deliveries, customer records, routes, payments, and monthly milk distribution data in one place.**

---

## The Problem

Managing daily milk deliveries manually can become difficult when there are multiple customers, different delivery locations, pending payments, and recurring orders to keep track of.

Common challenges include:

* **Managing Multiple Deliveries** — Keeping track of daily customers and delivery locations can become confusing.
* **Tracking Payments** — Pending and completed payments need to be recorded accurately.
* **Organizing Delivery Routes** — Delivery partners need a clear view of their stops and delivery sequence.
* **Maintaining Records** — Monthly milk quantities, customer details, and payment information can be difficult to maintain manually.
* **Handling Urgent Requests** — Important payment collections or delivery-related actions can easily be missed.
* **Generating Statements** — Preparing monthly delivery and payment summaries manually takes additional time.

**Dudh Sagar brings these everyday tasks together into a single organized system.**

---

## What Dudh Sagar Does

Dudh Sagar is designed to simplify the daily workflow of a dairy delivery partner by bringing delivery, customer, route, and payment management into one place.

* 📍 **Delivery Route Management** — View delivery stops and follow an organized route for daily deliveries.
* 🥛 **Daily Delivery Tracking** — Keep track of scheduled milk and dairy items for each customer.
* ➕ **Add Delivery Stops** — Add new customers with their address, scheduled items, and amount due.
* 💰 **Payment Tracking** — Monitor customer payments and outstanding amounts.
* ⚠️ **Urgent Payment Handling** — Quickly identify important payment collections and mark them as completed.
* 📊 **Monthly Ledger** — Maintain records of milk quantities, customer deliveries, payment amounts, and payment status.
* 🧾 **Monthly Statements** — View and print detailed delivery and payment summaries.
* 👤 **Profile & Route Management** — Update partner information, assigned route, phone number, and depot details.
* 🧭 **Simple Navigation** — Move easily between the dashboard, daily entries, history, settings, and login sections.

## Key Features

### 📍 Delivery Route

The route section provides a clear overview of delivery stops, including customer names, addresses, delivery status, and route sequence.

### 🥛 Daily Delivery Entry

New delivery stops can be added with customer details, delivery address, scheduled items, and the amount due.

### 💰 Payment Management

Payment information is connected with delivery records so that pending collections can be identified and completed deliveries can be updated accordingly.

### ⚠️ Urgent Actions

Important payment or delivery actions can be highlighted so they are easier to notice during the daily delivery routine.

### 📊 Monthly Ledger

The ledger provides a structured view of:

* Delivery dates
* Customer names
* Milk quantity
* Payment amounts
* Payment status

It also provides a summary of the total milk delivered and total amount collected.

### 🧾 Statement Generation

A detailed monthly statement can be viewed and printed, making it easier to maintain delivery and payment records.

### 👤 Partner Profile

Delivery partners can manage their basic information, assigned route, phone number, and depot or hub address.

## Why Dudh Sagar?

The goal of Dudh Sagar is to make everyday dairy delivery management **simpler, more organized, and easier to track**.

Instead of maintaining separate records for customers, deliveries, routes, and payments, everything can be accessed through a single workflow.

The system focuses on the practical needs of a delivery partner:

> **Know where to go → Know what to deliver → Track the payment → Maintain the record.**
